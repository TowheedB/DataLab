We study how structured knowledge — knowledge graphs, ontologies and their embeddings — can be combined with neural models. Recent work includes knowledge-graph embedding models for factoid entity questions, generating SPARQL queries from natural-language questions with triple-augmented language models, and adversarial multi-hop QA benchmarks grounded in knowledge graphs.
