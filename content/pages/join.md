cards:
- title: PhD & Master's students
  text: Graduate positions are available through the [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/programs/electrical-computer-engineering/) graduate program at TMU. A strong background in machine learning, NLP or information retrieval, and solid programming skills (Python, PyTorch) are expected. Prior research experience or publications are an asset.
- title: Undergraduate researchers
  text: TMU undergraduates can join for capstone projects, summer research or work-study positions. Recent projects have covered LLM bias measurement, question answering over knowledge graphs, study screening for systematic reviews and cloud-deployed web applications.
- title: Postdocs & visitors
  text: We occasionally host postdoctoral researchers and visiting scholars whose interests align with the lab's directions. Please reach out to discuss possible opportunities.
how_to_apply_title: How to apply
---
Email [fensan@torontomu.ca](mailto:fensan@torontomu.ca) with the subject line *"Prospective [PhD / MSc / undergrad] — Data Lab"*. Please include your CV, transcripts, a short statement of research interests, and — if applicable — links to publications, code or a thesis. Mention which of our [research directions](research.html) interests you most and why. Applications for graduate admission must also be submitted through TMU's official portal.
